WHO WE ARE

A Smarter Way to Manage Class Registration

ClassHub is a class registration platform designed to simplify the process of discovering and registering for academic courses. Students can explore available classes, review course information, check schedules, and manage their registrations through a centralized system.

Our goal is to reduce the complexity of traditional registration processes by providing an organized and user-friendly digital experience. The platform brings important class information together in one place so students can make informed academic decisions.
